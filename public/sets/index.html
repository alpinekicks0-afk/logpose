Sets

Every booster, extra booster, premium booster and starter deck that the cards on Log Pose are numbered in or pulled from, 47 set pages in all. The split is 18 boosters, 5 extra boosters, 2 premium boosters, and 22 starter decks. A set page lists the prize cards, promos and chase pulls tracked for that code, with the documented population where one was published. It is not a checklist of every card in the booster. A prize card keeps the number of the card it reprints, so an OP-09 number awarded at a 2025 Regional is on the OP-09 page. Together the set pages link 743 card pages.
