What the number can’t account for

Four regional Coliseums, two still to come
The English Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum runs in four regions — Europe, North America, South America and Oceania. Europe (Utrecht) and North America (Dallas) have been played; the South American and Oceanian events are confirmed but Bandai has not published their dates, so the total here projects all four and the running count covers the two played so far. [ffc26na]
Awarded by record, not by placement
Bandai gave this card to every player who finished the Swiss rounds undefeated (5-0 in Utrecht's 5 rounds, 6-0 in Dallas's 6 rounds). How many players did so is not published, so the number cannot be fixed; the ceiling is one per undefeated player across 4,096 Utrecht seats and 5,000 Dallas seats. [ffc26na]
Asia-English edition not counted
A separate Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum for the Asia-English region runs Nov 28–29, 2026 (Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur) with its own prize structure and region-stamped cards. It is a different card region and is not counted. [ffc26asia]
Other language regions
This page covers the English version (en.onepiece-cardgame.com). Asia-English, Japanese and Chinese championships use their own prize cards and are not included.
