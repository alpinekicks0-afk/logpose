What the number can’t account for

Four English copies in all, one per region
The English Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum trophy is printed for four regions — Europe, North America, South America and Oceania — and each is serial-numbered 1/1 for its own region, so four English copies exist in total, no two from the same region.
Sample image
Bandai's page shows a dummy/sample of the trophy card (a Flame-Flame Fruit card stamped 00/00 For Europe); the final print may differ. [ffc26eu]
Asia-English edition not counted
A separate Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum for the Asia-English region runs Nov 28–29, 2026 (Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur) with its own prize structure and region-stamped cards. It is a different card region and is not counted. [ffc26asia]
Other language regions
This page covers the English version (en.onepiece-cardgame.com). Asia-English, Japanese and Chinese championships use their own prize cards and are not included.
