What the number can’t account for

Store champions add an unpublished number
Every Extra Grand Battle for Stores 2026 May champion received one serial Yamato. Bandai does not publish the number of participating stores, so those copies are not in the count. [egbmay26]
Finals side-event prize wall (30 tickets)
Offered on the Championship 26-27 Finals Season 1 side-event prize wall at 30 tickets; quantity unpublished. [fin2627s1]
Placement tiers are counted as cumulative
Bandai lists prizes per placement tier. We count every player who reached a tier as receiving that tier's prize, and players who placed higher as receiving every lower tier's prize as well (a champion also collects the Top 8 and Top 16 prizes). If a tier was exclusive instead, the number is lower.
