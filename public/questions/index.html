Questions about One Piece TCG card populations

How the counts on this site are built, and the questions collectors ask most. Every figure links to the card page that shows its working.

What is the rarest ONE PIECE Card Game card?

By documented population the scarcest card tracked here is the Flame-Flame Fruit FFC 2026 NA (Champion), at 1/1. Several World Final and Coliseum trophy cards are one-of-one, awarded to a single champion and never sold.

How many textured finalist Luffy cards exist?

The P-033 Monkey.D.Luffy from the Championship 2023 Event Pack Finalist Ver. sits at ≈52+. Only Top 32 finishers at the 2023 Finals received the pack, and it held eleven textured cards, so the count is packs awarded divided across the card types.

What does a population count mean on Log Pose?

It is the number of copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's published event and product pages: events run, multiplied by the copies awarded at each placement. A plus sign means the figure is a floor — copies exist that cannot be counted, usually from store-run events whose host list Bandai never published.

Are these numbers the same as PSA or Beckett population reports?

No. Graded population reports count cards submitted for grading, and the same card can be counted twice if it is cracked and resubmitted. These figures count copies awarded at source, which is a different measure and usually a much smaller number.

What is a serial-numbered One Piece card?

A card printed with its own number on the face, such as 047/700. Bandai has used them for Regionals, Treasure Cup and World Final prizes. Because the run size is printed on the card itself, these are the only ONE PIECE cards with a population that is certain rather than reconstructed.

Which One Piece cards are one-of-one?

The World Final champion trophies from the 25-26 season onward are serial-numbered 1/1, as are the Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum regional champion trophies. Each exists in a single copy for its region or placement.
