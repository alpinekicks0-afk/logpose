Tiers

Prize tiers and chase rarities on Log Pose, 25 pages. A tier is the rarity or placement stored on the card, such as Champion, Finalist, Top 16 or SP Card. The largest are Champion (119), SP Card (114), Finalist (72), Top 16 (60), and Manga Rare (37). Each page lists every card filed under that label, rarest first. Tiers with fewer than 4 cards do not get their own URL; those cards stay on their card, set and event pages. Promo product names that share the same field, such as an anniversary set, are event pages rather than tiers.
