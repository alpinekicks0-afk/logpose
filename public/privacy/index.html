Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026.

Log Pose is a fan reference site kept by two collectors (see About). This page says, in plain words, what happens to information when you use logpose.cards.

Browsing without an account

You can read every page without signing in, and when you do we collect nothing that identifies you. The site has no advertising and no tracking or advertising cookies. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional and are for people aged 18 or over. An account stores only what is needed to make its features work:

  • Sign-in. You sign in with Google or Discord. We ask them only to confirm who you are, and we keep one thing from the answer: an ID number for your account with them, so we can recognise you next time. We never see your password. We do not ask for your email address, and we do not keep your name, picture or anything else Google or Discord may send along.
  • Your profile. The username you choose, whether your account is public or private, and an Instagram name and a references link if you add them.
  • Your lists. The cards on your wishlist and in your collection, with the grade you give each one.
  • Connections. Chat requests and your answers to them, friend requests and friends, accounts you have blocked, and the messages you send and receive in chats.
  • Reports. If you report an account, what you wrote and which account and chat it concerns.

Who can see your account

A private account cannot be found in search. Only friends you have accepted can see its wishlist, collection and links, and nobody is sent a chat request about cards it owns. A public account can be found by other signed-in members, who can see its wishlist, collection and links; when a card in a public collection is on someone's wishlist, both people get a chat request naming that card. Nothing in an account is shown to visitors who are not signed in, and accounts are kept out of search engines.

If you add a card to your wishlist, the public collectors who own it are sent a chat request. They see your username only if your account is public, you are friends, or you both accept.

Chats

A chat opens only when both people accept a request. Messages are stored in our database so they can be delivered; they are private to the two of you but are not end-to-end encrypted. We read a chat only when one of the two people in it reports it, in order to look into that report.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from its Profile tab. That removes your profile, lists, friends, blocks and chats, including the other person's copy of each chat. Reports you sent or received are kept without your account attached.

Cookies and the captcha

Signing in sets cookies that keep you signed in for up to 30 days. They are used for nothing else and are removed when you sign out. Creating an account requires a captcha, Cloudflare Turnstile, to confirm you are a person; Cloudflare checks signals from your browser and your IP address to do that. Account information is stored in a Cloudflare database.

What is saved on your device

The site remembers three display choices — how lists are sorted and which view you last used — in your browser's local storage, so they are still set when you come back. They stay on your device, are never sent to us, and disappear if you clear your browser's site data.

Hosting and visitor statistics

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare's servers handle technical details of each request — such as IP address, browser type and the page requested — in order to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare also gives us aggregate statistics, such as how many visits a page had and which countries or sites they came from. Those statistics do not let us identify individual visitors and are not used for advertising. Cloudflare's own policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Fonts and outside links

The interactive ledger loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google's servers. Card pages also link to outside sites, including Bandai's official pages and Instagram; once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Changes and contact

If the site ever starts collecting anything more, this page will be updated before that happens. Questions: Message either of the two collectors who keep the site on Instagram: @alpine.cards or @davy_d_xebec_.
