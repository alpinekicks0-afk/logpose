Champion cards

Log Pose files 119 cards under Champion. On each card page the rarity or tier field is Champion. All 119 have a documented print-run population. Documented figures run from 1 to 41. They are filed under Special Tournaments, Serialized Cards, World Finals, National Finals, and Regionals. The lowest documented population is Flame-Flame Fruit FFC 2026 EU (Champion) at 1/1. A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded. This page lists every card with that tier, rarest first, and links to the card page for the working.
