SP Card cards

Log Pose files 120 cards under SP Card. On each card page the rarity or tier field is SP Card. None of them has a documented print-run population; all 120 are unpublished. They are filed under Main Sets. A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded. This page lists every card with that tier, rarest first, and links to the card page for the working.
