3rd Place cards

Log Pose files 7 cards under 3rd Place. On each card page the rarity or tier field is 3rd Place. All 7 have a documented print-run population. Documented figures run from 1 to 8. They are filed under Serialized Cards, World Finals, and National Finals. The lowest documented population is Monkey.D.Luffy CS 25-26 WF (3rd Place) at 1/1. A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded.
