Top Player Pack cards

Log Pose files 35 cards under Top Player Pack. On each card page the rarity or tier field is Top Player Pack. All 35 have a documented print-run population. Documented figures run from 42 to 128. They are filed under National Finals. The lowest documented population is Buggy ST17-003 (Top Player Pack) at ≈42+. A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded.
