Finalist cards

Log Pose files 72 cards under Finalist. On each card page the rarity or tier field is Finalist. All 72 have a documented print-run population. Documented figures run from 192 to 1,312. They are filed under Regionals. The lowest documented population is Adio P-078 (Finalist) at 192+. A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded. This page lists every card with that tier, rarest first, and links to the card page for the working.
