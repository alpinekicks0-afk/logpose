OP-04 Kingdoms of Intrigue

OP-04 (Kingdoms of Intrigue) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 19 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: September 22, 2023. 6 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Capone"Gang"Bege OP04-100 (Champion) at 8+. 13 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (9), Champion (2), Chinese-exclusive Treasure Rare (2), and 2nd ANNIVERSARY SET (JP) (1). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
