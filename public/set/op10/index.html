OP-10 Royal Blood

OP-10 (Royal Blood) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 35 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: March 21, 2025. 13 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Tashigi OP10-032 (Champion) at 3+. 22 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (11), Champion (4), Top 16 (4), and Top Player Pack (4). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
