OP-17 The World's Strongest Warriors

OP-17 (The World's Strongest Warriors) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 29 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: August 28, 2026. 1 of them has a documented print-run population, the lowest being Benn.Beckman OP17-027 (Top 16) at 0+. 28 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (13), Manga Rare (4), Special alternate art (3), and Crew alternate art (2). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
