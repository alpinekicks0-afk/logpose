OP-14 prize and chase cards

OP-14 on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 23 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. In this log, OP-14 is released with EB-04 on January 16, 2026. 13 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Donquixote Doflamingo OP14-069 (Champion) at 3+. 10 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (5), Champion (3), Event Manga (2), and Finalist (2). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
