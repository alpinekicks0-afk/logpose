OP-05 Awakening of the New Era

OP-05 (Awakening of the New Era) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 44 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: December 8, 2023. 14 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Monkey.D.Luffy OP05-119 (Champion) at 1+. 30 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (12), Anime 25th leader parallel (5), Champion (3), and Manga Rare (3). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
