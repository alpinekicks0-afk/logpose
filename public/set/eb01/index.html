EB-01 Memorial Collection

EB-01 (Memorial Collection) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that extra booster, 21 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: May 3, 2024. 11 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Kid & Killer EB01-003 (Champion) at 6+. 10 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are Champion (4), Finalist (4), SP Card (4), and Anime 25th leader parallel (3). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
