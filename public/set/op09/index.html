OP-09 Emperors in the New World

OP-09 (Emperors in the New World) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 69 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: December 13, 2024. 19 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Nico Olvia OP09-106 (Champion) at 6+. 50 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (11), 2nd Anniversary Set (EN) (7), Top 8 (5), and Wanted-poster SP (5). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
