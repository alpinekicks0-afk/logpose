PRB-02 ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST vol.2

PRB-02 (ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST vol.2) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that premium booster, 18 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: October 3, 2025. 2 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Monkey.D.Luffy PRB02-005 (Top 32) at 32+. 16 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (8), Event Manga (4), Chinese-exclusive Treasure Rare (1), and Manga Rare (1). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
