OP-06 Wings of the Captain

OP-06 (Wings of the Captain) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 47 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: March 15, 2024. 22 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Nekomamushi OP06-110 (Champion) at 7+. 25 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (12), Champion (7), Anime 25th leader parallel (6), and Top 16 (6). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
