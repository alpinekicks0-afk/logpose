OP-18 The Dominance of God

OP-18 (The Dominance of God) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 16 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: November 20, 2026. None of them has a documented print-run population. All 16 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (6), Special alternate art (4), Crew alternate art (1), and Event Manga (1). A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded.
