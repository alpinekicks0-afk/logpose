OP-13 Carrying On His Will

OP-13 (Carrying On His Will) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 53 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: November 7, 2025. 16 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Saint Charlos OP13-087 (Champion) at 3+. 37 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (11), Demon Pack SSP (5), Top 16 (5), and Wanted-poster SP (4). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
