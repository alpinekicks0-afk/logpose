ST-18 Monkey.D.Luffy

ST-18 (Monkey.D.Luffy) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that starter deck, 5 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. 1 of them has a documented print-run population, the lowest being San-Gorou ST18-003 (Top 64) at 704+. 4 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (3), Top 64 (1), and Treasure Rare (1). A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded.
