OP-02 Paramount War

OP-02 (Paramount War) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 32 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: March 10, 2023. 24 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Mr.1 (Daz.Bonez) OP02-063 (Champion) at 7+. 8 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are Champion (8), Finalist (8), SP Card (5), and Top 64 (4). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
