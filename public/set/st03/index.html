ST-03 prize and chase cards

ST-03 on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that starter deck, 15 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. 12 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Trafalgar Law ST03-008 (Champion) at 8+. 3 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are Champion (5), Finalist (4), Chinese-exclusive Treasure Rare (1), and SP Card (1). A population here is the copies that can be accounted for from Bandai's event and product pages. It is not a PSA or Beckett census, which only counts cards that were sent in to be graded.
