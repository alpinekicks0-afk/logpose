OP-03 Pillars of Strength

OP-03 (Pillars of Strength) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 42 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: June 30, 2023. 29 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Brannew OP03-089 (Champion) at 7+. 13 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are Champion (12), Finalist (12), SP Card (6), and Wanted-poster SP (4). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
