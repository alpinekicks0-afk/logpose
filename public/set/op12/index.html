OP-12 Legacy of the Master

OP-12 (Legacy of the Master) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 32 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: August 22, 2025. 7 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Charlotte Pudding OP12-071 (Champion) at 33+. 25 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (9), 3rd ANNIVERSARY SET (JP) (5), Champion (2), and Event Manga (2). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
