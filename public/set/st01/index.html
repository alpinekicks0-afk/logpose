ST-01 Straw Hat Crew

ST-01 (Straw Hat Crew) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that starter deck, 40 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. 19 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Gum-Gum Jet Pistol ST01-015 (Champion) at 7+. 21 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are Champion (7), 25th Anniversary Edition (5), Finalist (4), and 1st Anniversary Set (EN) (3). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
