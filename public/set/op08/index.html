OP-08 Two Legends

OP-08 (Two Legends) on Log Pose is every prize card, promo and chase pull tracked for that booster, 32 cards in all. It is not a checklist of the whole set. Release date on file: September 13, 2024. 11 of them have a documented print-run population, the lowest being Drum Kingdom OP08-020 (Champion) at 8+. 21 are unpublished, because the printing is confirmed and no print run was published. The tiers on this page are SP Card (8), Anime 25th leader parallel (6), Top 16 (4), and Champion (3). Each card links to the page with the event-by-event working and the official sources.
