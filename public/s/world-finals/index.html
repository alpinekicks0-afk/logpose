World Finals

The World Final in Japan crowns the global champion each March. Bandai awards the 1st (gold), 2nd (silver) and 3rd (bronze) place trophy cards, one of each in the world, plus a commemorative World Champion DON!! card for every competitor (player counts are unpublished, so the DON!! cards carry no number). The 26-27 World Final has not been announced.
