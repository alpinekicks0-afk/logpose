Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum 2026 (BCG Fest Utrecht Sep 4 · Dallas Sep 19–20)

Two Coliseums, each split into four blocks. The Champion of each region took home a serial-numbered Flame-Flame Fruit trophy card — one for Europe, one for North America. Block placements are counted ×4; the Swiss-record cards (Law, Usopp, Sabo) and the DON!! card went out by win record and carry no number.

Super Pre-Release Event 2022 (store launch week, September 30 – October 6, 2022)

The English game's first tournament, played at local stores with the Super Pre-Release starter decks. Every store champion received the silver-foil P-001 Monkey.D.Luffy; every entrant the regular P-001. Bandai published neither store count nor attendance, so both carry no number.
